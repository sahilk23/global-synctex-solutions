03How we work

One contact from tech pack to shipment

You deal with one person who knows your brief. We handle the mills, the follow-up and the paperwork.

  1. Share your brief

    Send a tech pack, a swatch or a spec line: composition, GSM, width, finish, quantity and target date.

  2. Get a shortlist

04Sourcing insights

Notes from the sourcing desk

Short pieces from our founder on what buyers and Indian mills need from each other. Full posts are on LinkedIn.

For buyers & mills

The mill you need exists. It just doesn’t have a website.

Search the open web for warp-knit tricot and you find East Asian mills with GSM, width, MOQ and test reports. Indian units in Surat and Ludhiana make the same cloth, but almost none publish it. India loses orders on missing evidence, not on skill.

Buying strategy

Do global buyers still need a sourcing agent?

Yes, but only a specialist. Buyers are spreading sourcing across countries while cutting supplier lists. They need someone who makes India less risky: the right mill for the spec, samples and test reports on time, and checks before shipment.

Market outlook

Outdoor technical fabrics, 2026 to 2036

PFAS-free water repellency becomes the minimum standard. Digital product records and recycling data enter every buying decision. Single-material, recyclable and bio-based fabrics move from trials to scale.

Product development

One puffer, three versions

A basic stand-collar puffer, a design-detail version and a hooded warmer one share the same core skills: quilting, fill and a down-proof shell. One Indian factory can develop all three for a buyer from a single programme.
